What you’ll be able to do

Know what makes a skillseparate a task that repeats from a one-time case, a general rule, and changing information.
A working skill of your owncreated in a conversation with Claude, uploaded to the app, and used for a common request.
Test and fixfour test requests, with and without the skill, and the fix in the right place.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if you

  • Already use Claude in your browser and repeat the same instructions every week.
  • Work with customer service, documents, and clients, like Raul the lawyer and Débora, the pet shop owner.
  • Have little time and no technical background.
  • Prefer learning by doing.

It’s not for you if you

  • Already know the subject well and want something technical.
  • Want traditional video lessons.
